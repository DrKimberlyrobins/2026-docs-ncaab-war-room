How these scores are calculated

Scheduled updates retrieve the public KenPom ratings table and ESPN’s daily schedule. The same prepared results load on every device. No API key, paste box or manual ratings entry is required. First-half projections combine the full-game matchup estimate with each team’s observed first-half scoring share and its opponent’s first-half allowing share. These shares use actual regulation half scores and exclude overtime.

Expected possessions = both teams’ adjusted tempos added, minus the configured national tempo. Matchup efficiency = team offense + opponent defense − configured national efficiency. Points = possessions × matchup efficiency ÷ 100.

The national efficiency and tempo baselines are the dated preseason configuration shown above, not a live national-average feed. Home-court effects and injuries are not modeled. First-half and full-game projections are unvalidated; no win probability is inferred. Each first-half timing profile requires at least three completed games. Prior-season profiles are labeled when current-season samples are insufficient. Unmatched teams remain visible as unavailable. Retrieval-time calculations are not certified pregame forecasts.
