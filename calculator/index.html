How the calculator works

Possessions = Team A tempo + Team B tempo − national tempo. Matchup efficiency = team offense + opponent defense − national efficiency. Points = possessions × matchup efficiency ÷ 100.

Automatic national averages are dated configuration, not a live national-average feed. Home advantage is currently configured at zero; home-court effects, injuries and lineups are not modeled. Full-game output is an unvalidated baseline. KenPom's own score projection appears separately for comparison. No first-half score or win probability is manufactured from these inputs.

Refreshes retain timestamped snapshots. Retrieval time alone does not establish that data was captured before tipoff. Missing API access or missing team ratings stays visible.
