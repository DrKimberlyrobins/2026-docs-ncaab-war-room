How these scores are calculated

Scheduled updates retrieve the public KenPom ratings table and ESPN’s daily schedule. The same prepared results load on every device. No API key, paste box or manual ratings entry is required.

Expected possessions = both teams’ adjusted tempos added, minus the configured national tempo. Matchup efficiency = team offense + opponent defense − configured national efficiency. Points = possessions × matchup efficiency ÷ 100.

The national efficiency and tempo baselines are the dated preseason configuration shown above, not a live national-average feed. Home-court effects and injuries are not modeled. These are unvalidated full-game projections; no first-half score or win probability is inferred. Unmatched teams remain visible as unavailable. Retrieval-time calculations are not certified pregame forecasts.
