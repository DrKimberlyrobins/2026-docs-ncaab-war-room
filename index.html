What powers this room

Sources & coverage

ESPN public team directory, scoreboards, team schedules and game summaries. Source links and retrieval times accompany analysis. ESPN group 50 is used for Division I discovery; the returned membership has not been reconciled against the NCAA roster. A team count is not a coverage certificate.

Transparent scoring baseline · v1

For each team, use up to 10 completed games strictly before tipoff from the selected season. Expected away points = (away points scored + home points allowed) ÷ 2; expected home points = (home points scored + away points allowed) ÷ 2. No arbitrary home advantage is added. Total = both projections added; home margin = home minus away. When team schedules omit period scores, the three most recent completed game summaries supply actual halves. First-half scoring uses actual first-period scores only, never a fixed fraction of a full-game score. At least three scored games per team are required for either period.

This is an uncalibrated descriptive baseline, not a validated prediction model. No win probabilities, confidence grades or green betting qualifications are assigned. Schedule strength, possessions, roster changes, availability and market pricing are not modeled. Prior-season mode is labeled and does not establish current player roles. Overtime is included in full-game historical scores.

Markets & players

Market lines appear only when supplied by the scoreboard. No fabricated odds. Men's games have two halves; there is no first-quarter market here. Game summaries show reported injuries, probable/confirmed lineup information only when the feed provides it, and actual player box scores. Missing starting fives, expected minutes, rotation pairings and player prop lines remain DATA UNAVAILABLE.

Receipts & honest grading

Prior-season reference baselines can be saved with their season label and roster-continuity warning. Save is available only before scheduled tipoff and before live play. Records include the exact baseline, source sample, season, source timestamps, market information and model version. Grading reports actual scores and absolute score errors. It does not call exceeding a projection a winning wager. Historical analyses cannot be saved as pregame forecasts. Browser clock and storage are not a tamper-proof public archive.

Quality over quantity. Missing data stays missing.
